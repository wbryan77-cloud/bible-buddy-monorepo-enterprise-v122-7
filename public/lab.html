How to use this Lab

        

          Think of this as your “workshop” with Bible Buddy. You experiment, and the AI learns what works for           real testers.         

        

Good first things to try

        
              
  • Outline a single devotional on a verse you love.
    •           
  • Plan a 3–5 day mini-series for a Bible study group.
    •           
  • Turn messy sermon notes into a clear outline.
    •           
  • Ask for reflection questions + a short closing prayer.
    •         
        

Bible Buddy focuses on

        
              
  • Scripture-rooted encouragement and clarity.
    •           
  • Helping you organize thoughts into steps, not replacing you.
    •           
  • Staying kind, gentle, and grounded in God’s Word.
    •         
        

Tester notes for Admin

        

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

        
              
  • Did Bible Buddy understand your request?
    •           
  • Was the outline or answer actually useful to you?
    •           
  • Did anything feel confusing, too slow, or “off”?
    •         
        

          Add thoughts in “Tester notes” so Admin can see patterns and improve the experience over time.
