How to use this Lab

Think of this as your “workshop” with Bible Buddy.

You experiment, and the AI learns what works for real testers.

Good first things to try

  • Outline a single devotional on a verse you love (add cross-references).
  • Plan a 3–5 day mini-series for a Bible study group.
  • Turn messy sermon notes into a clear, Scripture-anchored outline.
  • Ask for reflection questions plus a short closing prayer.

Bible Buddy focuses on

  • Scripture-rooted encouragement and clarity.
  • Helping you organize thoughts into steps, not replacing you.
  • Staying kind, gentle, and grounded in God’s Word.

Tester notes for Admin

What to look for:

  • Did Bible Buddy understand your request?
  • Was the outline or answer actually useful to you?
  • Did anything feel confusing, too slow, or “off”?

Add thoughts in your “Tester notes” area so Admin can see patterns and improve the experience over time.
