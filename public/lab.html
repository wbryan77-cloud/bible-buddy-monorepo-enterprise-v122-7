How to use this Lab
              

                Think of this as your “workshop” with Bible Buddy. You experiment, and the AI                 learns what works for real testers.               

              
                    
  • Outline a single devotional on a verse you love.
    •                 
  • Plan a 3–5 day mini-series for a Bible study group.
    •                 
  • Turn messy sermon notes into a clear outline.
    •                 
  • Ask for reflection questions + a short closing prayer.
