# Alpha Tester Agreement (Draft)

**Status:** Product/testing agreement draft — not legal advice. Recommend attorney review before broad external testing.

## 1. Confidential Beta

BibleBuddy is a confidential alpha/beta application under active development. You agree not to copy, reverse engineer, redistribute, resell, or publicly share screenshots, recordings, or access links without written permission.

## 2. Approved Testers Only

Do not share the app link outside approved testers. Do not use confidential access to build or assist a competing product.

## 3. Feedback License

You grant permission to use feedback, bug reports, and quality observations to improve the product. Feedback may be incorporated without compensation.

## 4. Conversation Review

You consent that conversations may be reviewed for quality, safety, and product improvement. By default, stored data uses message **previews** (not full transcripts unless explicitly enabled).

## 5. Privacy

Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information (addresses, financial data, medical diagnoses, etc.). The app stores limited tester profile fields and bounded session previews.

## 6. No Professional Advice

BibleBuddy is **not** a pastor, therapist, doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor. Scripture content is for study and spiritual support. For emergencies, contact appropriate professionals or emergency services.

## 7. Experimental Software

The app may make mistakes. Features may change or break during alpha testing.

## 8. Stop Testing

You may stop testing at any time. Request deletion of stored tester profile where applicable.

## 9. Confidentiality Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive at least **three (3) years** or until public release, whichever is later.

## 10. Intellectual Property

All software, doctrine authority systems, and product IP remain with BibleBuddy / Jacob12 / configured owner entity.
