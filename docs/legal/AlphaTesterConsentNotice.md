# Alpha Tester Consent Notice

## What you are joining

An **experimental alpha** of BibleBuddy — a Scripture-grounded AI companion. This is not a public release.

## What we collect

- Basic profile (name, optional contact, age range, device, timezone, Bible familiarity)
- Session previews (user message and Buddy reply previews, routing metadata)
- Optional in-chat feedback tags (helpful, wrong Scripture, etc.)
- Notification preferences

## What we do not collect by default

- Full raw transcripts (unless `ALPHA_CAPTURE_FULL_TRANSCRIPTS=true` on server)
- Secrets, passwords, or payment data
- Full OpenAI payloads or full runtime debug objects

## How data is used

- Improve answer quality, safety, and companion behavior
- Diagnose bugs and doctrine routing issues
- Aggregate issue reports for engineering

## Your responsibilities

- Do not share the app link or screenshots publicly
- Avoid highly sensitive personal details in chat
- Understand Buddy is not professional counseling or emergency services

## Opt out

Pause notifications in settings. Contact the project owner to request profile removal.
