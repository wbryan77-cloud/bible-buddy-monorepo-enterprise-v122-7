Phase 6H, Part 10. This review re-validates the Phase 6F competitive
audit (`docs/alpha/phase6f-20260719-075444/CompetitivePositioning2026.md`

chase.

## The one fact that matters most

YouVersion (1B+ downloads, category leader) has publicly declined to ship
open-ended AI Bible chat, citing independent findings that current AI
models misquote Scripture 15–60% of the time. BibleBuddy's entire
retrieval architecture — Scripture is retrieved from a governed local KJV
corpus, never generated by a language model — is a direct, provable
answer to the exact concern the market leader raised. This remains
BibleBuddy's strongest, most defensible differentiator and should stay
the headline claim in any Founder-facing materials.

**Finding: every advertised differentiator that a Founder would actually
see in the running app is real and currently working, not just claimed
in a diff-strip.** This is the single most important thing this review
found — the marketing claim and the shipped product match.

## Where the Founder-visible product could visibly fall short of a first impression, and the smallest fix

1. **Lesson Alignment had no Admin-side visibility until this pass** — a
   Founder could use the tool, but nothing was retained for an Admin to
   review afterward, undermining the "governed" story for this one
   feature specifically. **Fixed this pass**: submissions are now logged
   (append-only, same pattern as every other audit trail in this repo)
   and visible in the Admin Founder Readiness tab.
2. **Two of the twelve named orb states (Original Language, Historical
   Context) were not yet wired to real transitions** — the orb is the
   product's visual identity and a Founder who explicitly asks a Hebrew/
   Greek word-study question would have seen a generic "speaking" pulse
   instead of a state that reflects what's actually happening. **Fixed
   this pass** (Part 2) — deterministic, content-driven, no guessing.
3. **No small, aggregate usage signal existed to tell the Founder team
   which differentiators testers actually engage with** (witness
   retrieval vs. original language vs. prayer vs. lesson alignment) —
   without this, "improve BibleBuddy after Founder Alpha" (Part 7's
   stated purpose) would have no data to work from on day one. **Fixed
   this pass** — Founder Observation Layer, aggregate-only, no message
   content or identity stored.

None of these were feature gaps versus competitors — they were Founder-
visibility gaps in an already-differentiated product. No feature-parity
work (multi-translation library, audio Bible, reading-plan depth, social
layer) is recommended for this phase, consistent with the Phase 6F
"defer" list, which remains correct.

## Recommendation

**Ship as-is for Founder Alpha.** The differentiation is real,
provable, and — after this pass — fully visible end-to-end (a Founder can
see the witness, the lineage, the original language, the historical
label, and now also verify Lesson Alignment was actually reviewable by
an Admin). No further competitive-positioning work is required before
Founder Alpha begins.
