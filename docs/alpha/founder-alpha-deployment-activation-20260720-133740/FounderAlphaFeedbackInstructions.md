# BibleBuddy Founder Alpha — How to Give Feedback

## The Fastest Way

Use the in-app feedback option while you're in BibleBuddy. It's tied to your Founder tester
session automatically, so we can follow up on the exact conversation if needed.

## What Makes Feedback Most Useful to Us

When something feels wrong, confusing, or broken, please tell us:

1. **What you asked** (the exact question or message, if you can recall/paste it).
2. **What you expected** to happen or hear.
3. **What actually happened** instead.
4. **How serious it felt to you** — see the severity guide below.

## Severity Guide

- **Critical:** BibleBuddy gave an answer that misquotes or contradicts Scripture, gave harmful
  guidance in a prayer/emotional-support/decision-support conversation, or exposed
  private/technical information it shouldn't have.
- **High:** A core feature (Scripture lookup, companion chat, Lesson Alignment) doesn't work as
  expected, or the app is very slow/unresponsive.
- **Medium:** Something is confusing, awkward, or slightly inaccurate but not dangerous or
  doctrine-breaking.
- **Low:** Cosmetic issues, wording suggestions, minor UI polish.

If you believe you've found a **Critical** issue, please also email/message
[SUPPORT_CONTACT] directly in addition to in-app feedback, so we can look at it right away.

## Other Ways to Reach Us

Reply to your invitation email, or contact [SUPPORT_CONTACT].

## Thank You

read all of it.
