# BibleBuddy Founder Alpha — Privacy Notice

**Testing period:** 14 days from your invitation date.

## What We Actually Collect

Based on the telemetry actually implemented in BibleBuddy today (verified in this batch's Part 9
review), during Founder Alpha we may collect and review:

- A session/tester identifier you're given (not your name, not an account — just a code used to
  keep your testing activity separate from other testers').
- The questions and messages you send, and the responses BibleBuddy gives (needed to check
  Scripture accuracy and improve answer quality).
- Basic technical metadata: which feature you used (e.g., original-language study, Lesson
  Alignment), how long a response took, whether an error occurred, and which internal "route" a
  response came from (used only to diagnose problems, not to build a profile of you).
- Any feedback you explicitly submit through the in-app feedback option.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not log your keystrokes.
- We do not monitor your device, your browser activity outside the app, or any other app.
- We do not collect passwords (BibleBuddy Founder Alpha has no login/password system).
- We do not knowingly collect financial, medical, or other highly sensitive personal information —
  please avoid sharing this in chat, since chat content may be reviewed by our team.

## Who Can See This Information

Only the authorized BibleBuddy Founder/development team. This information is never sold, and never
made public or shared with any third party outside that team.

## Why We Collect It

Solely to improve BibleBuddy during this limited Founder Alpha testing period — checking Scripture
accuracy, fixing bugs, understanding which features are useful, and responding to your feedback.

## How Long We Keep It

For the duration of the Founder Alpha testing period and a reasonable period afterward needed to
act on feedback, unless you request earlier deletion (see below).
