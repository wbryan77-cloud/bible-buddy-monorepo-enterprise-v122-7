## Welcome to BibleBuddy Founder Alpha

You're one of a small group of Founder testers helping shape BibleBuddy before it opens more
broadly. Thank you for being here.

**A few things to know before you start:**

- BibleBuddy answers directly from Scripture wherever possible, and shows its sources (verses,
  supporting witnesses, cross-references) so you can check its work.
- This is an early build. You may find rough edges — that's exactly what this testing period is
  for.
- Your conversations, feedback, and basic usage information may be reviewed by our small
  development team to improve BibleBuddy. See the Privacy Notice for full detail.
- Please avoid sharing highly sensitive personal, financial, medical, or password information in
  chat.
- Use the feedback option anytime something feels wrong, confusing, or broken — we read every
  report.

**Testing period:** 14 days.

We're grateful you're testing this with us. Let's begin.
