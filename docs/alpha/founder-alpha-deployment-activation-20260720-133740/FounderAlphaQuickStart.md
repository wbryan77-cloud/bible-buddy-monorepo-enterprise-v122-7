## 2. Try These First

- **Ask a direct Scripture question:** "What does John 3:16 say?"
- **Ask a doctrine question:** "What does the Bible say about the Sabbath?"
- **Ask for prayer:** "Can you pray with me about something I'm anxious about?"
- **Go deeper:** "What's the original Greek behind John 1:1?" or "What's the historical context of
  Psalm 23?"
- **Have a real conversation:** ask a follow-up question, change topics, come back later — see how
  it remembers and adapts.

## 3. If You Teach or Lead a Group

Try the Scripture Alignment tool: paste a short lesson or sermon excerpt and see how BibleBuddy
checks its quotations against the King James Bible and flags anything that doesn't match.

## 4. Give Feedback

Use the in-app feedback option whenever something feels wrong, confusing, inaccurate, or broken.
Be as specific as you can: what you asked, what you expected, what actually happened.

## 5. Please Don't

- Share highly sensitive personal, financial, medical, or password information in chat.
- Share your Founder Testing URL publicly — it's meant for you and the other invited Founders
  only, during this 14-day testing period.
