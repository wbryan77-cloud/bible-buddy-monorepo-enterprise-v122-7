> literalGloss is deterministically the FIRST entry in Strong's own kjv_def field for that lemma. Strong's orders kjv_def alphabetically, not by contextual frequency, so a well-attested lemma (e.g. H0430 "Elohim") can surface an atypical first gloss (e.g. "angels" before "God") on a verse where the contextual meaning is the far more common sense. This is genuine vendored dictionary data, never invented, but it can look misleading without this note.
