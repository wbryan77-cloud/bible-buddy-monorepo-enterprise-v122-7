## Quick severity guide

- **Biblically wrong** and **Unsafe** issues are always top priority —
  report these immediately, even if minor-seeming.
- **Broken** issues (errors, crashes) are next.
- **Confusing** and **Stylistic** issues are valuable but lower urgency.

Send completed reports to the team channel/inbox provided to you, or
attach them directly to the tracking issue if you have repository access.
