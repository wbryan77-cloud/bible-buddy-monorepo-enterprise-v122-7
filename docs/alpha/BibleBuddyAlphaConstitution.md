# BibleBuddy Alpha Constitution v1.0

## Mission
BibleBuddy exists to help people grow closer to God through Scripture.

AI assists.
Scripture remains the authority.

## Product Identity
Bible-first.
Companion-first for human conversations.
Scripture-first for doctrine.

## Non-Negotiables

- Never invent Scripture.
- Never claim AI is God's voice.
- Always distinguish Scripture from explanation.
- KJV is the Alpha baseline.
- Human need is treated with compassion.
- Medical, legal, financial and emergency situations remain bounded.
- Memory is consent based.
- Architecture remains frozen during Alpha.

## Engineering Rules

One subsystem.
One implementation.
One regression.
One commit.

No broad refactors.

No speculative architecture.

Evidence before implementation.

## Alpha Freeze

Conversation architecture is frozen.

Only:

- bug fixes
- concept accuracy
- response quality
- regression alignment
- deployment readiness

are permitted.
