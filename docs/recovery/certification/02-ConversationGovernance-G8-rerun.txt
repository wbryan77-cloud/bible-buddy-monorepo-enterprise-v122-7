REPLY According to Scripture, the heavens can be understood in layered ways. The first heaven refers to the firmament or sky, as seen in Genesis 1:6-8, where God separated the waters and created the firmament. The second heaven typically refers to the celestial realm, where the stars and planets reside, detailed in Genesis 1:14-17. The third heaven is described in 2 Corinthians 12:2, where Paul speaks of being caught up to the third heaven; however, this does not imply that all believers ascend there upon death. Notably, John 3:13 states that no man has ascended to heaven except the Son of Man. Regarding the second coming, Scripture emphasizes that Jesus Christ will return to establish His kingdom on earth. Matthew 6:10 expresses a prayer for God’s kingdom to come and be established on earth, while Acts 1:11 reassures that Jesus will return in the same manner as He ascended. The culmination of His return is depicted in Revelation 21:1-3, where a new heaven and new earth are described, signifying God's eternal dwelling with humanity. This understanding emphasizes the layered nature of the heavens and the promise of Christ's return to reign on earth.
