# ADR-003 — Authority Domains

## Status

Accepted and permanent.

## Domains

### SAFETY

Crisis, immediate danger, abuse, self-harm, emergency, and safety intervention.

### SCRIPTURE

Doctrine, Bible references, Scripture requests, Bible-wide comparison, claim
validation, and Scripture-silence decisions.

### COMPANION

Emotional support, listening, prayer companionship, health-support
conversation, practical guidance, relationship support, app identity, and
ordinary conversation.

## Decision rule

The user's request determines the authority domain.

Concept recognition assists execution but does not decide whether Scripture
owns the request.
