# ADR-005 — Final Response Owner

## Status

Accepted and permanent.

## Decision

One final response owner delivers every user-visible reply.

Intended chain:

finalizeBuddyResponse
→ liveResponseOwner
→ singleCompanionContract

The final owner may polish wording but may not alter canonical Scripture,
references, doctrine polarity, truth decisions, Scripture-silence decisions,
or the previously assigned authority domain.
