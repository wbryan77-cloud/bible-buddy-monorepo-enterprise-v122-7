# BibleBuddy — Governed Q&A Candidates from BookBuddy Source Reconciliation

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: CANDIDATE ONLY — NO AUTO-PROMOTION

These questions were generated from gaps, tensions, and reader questions surfaced by the restored BookBuddy corpus and the five-book reconciliation. They are not doctrine answers. Each requires the established Scripture-first pipeline, countertext review, and human approval before promotion.

## Candidate questions

1. **What is the difference between biblical self-control and Stoic detachment?**
   - Compare biblical dependence, prayer, duty, love, lament, and hope with the Stoic control distinction.
   - Do not caricature Stoicism or import it as biblical authority.

2. **Does the Bible teach that I should focus only on what I can control?**
   - Examine prayer, providence, responsibility, surrender, intercession, community obligation, and passages about anxiety/control.
   - Distinguish a useful psychological heuristic from a complete biblical doctrine.

3. **How should a Christian grieve without pretending pain is unbelief?**
   - Scripture chain should include lament, Jesus weeping, resurrection hope, grief with hope, bodily weakness, community presence, and patience.
   - Psychological/clinical grief material is supplemental only.

4. **How can suffering have meaning when God does not explain why it happened?**
   - Distinguish meaning, purpose, redemption, consequence, mystery, and invented causal explanations.
   - Do not universalize Job, Romans 8:28, or restoration promises beyond their text.

5. **What does Paul mean by another Jesus, another spirit, and another gospel in 2 Corinthians 11?**
   - Read the whole chapter and connect carefully with deception, false apostles, Satan appearing as an angel of light, and ministers appearing righteous.
   - Build Genesis-to-Revelation countertexts; do not use the passage as a label for every disagreement.

6. **How should I test a pastor, prophet, teacher, or spiritual feeling without becoming cynical?**
   - Include testing spirits, fruit, Scripture/context, elder accountability, false prophets, humility, correction, and safeguards against gossip/talebearing.
   - Distinguish church discipline from public-safety/legal duties.

7. **What is Acts 10 actually about, and what does it prove or not prove about clean and unclean food?**
   - Read Acts 10–11, especially Peter's stated application to people, then compare Mark 7, Romans 14, 1 Timothy 4, Leviticus 11, Deuteronomy 14, and relevant prophetic texts.
   - Do not let one proof text settle the entire chain without countertext review.

8. **Does Scripture ever explicitly change the seventh-day Sabbath to Sunday?**
   - Trace Genesis, Exodus, prophets, Jesus, Acts, Hebrews, first-day texts, Romans 14, Colossians 2, and Revelation.
   - Separate worship on a day from a claimed transfer of Sabbath identity; present major Christian counter-readings fairly.

9. **What do the appointed times in Leviticus 23 point to across Scripture?**
   - Passover, Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, Pentecost, Trumpets, Atonement, Tabernacles, Eighth Day.
   - Label explicit New Testament connections separately from thematic/typological synthesis.
   - Examine Zechariah 14 and future-continuity questions without assuming future occurrence alone settles present obligation.

10. **How do I distinguish explicit Scripture, biblical synthesis, inference, history, and tradition?**
    - Build a reader-facing explanation and examples.
    - Include examples where a familiar teaching may be reasonable but is not stated verbatim in the text.

11. **Can a philosophy or psychology idea be useful and still be wrong as a final authority?**
    - Compare wisdom/observation with revelation and biblical authority.
    - Use public-domain philosophy and modern psychology as examples only; Scripture remains controlling.

12. **How should Christians respond when church hurt makes someone want to leave religion?**
    - Distinguish rejecting God, rejecting a false claim about God, reacting to abuse/hypocrisy, unresolved pain, doctrinal confusion, and desire for autonomy.
    - Emphasize safety, truth, repentance, accountability, patient listening, and invitation to examine Scripture directly.

## Required evaluation dimensions

For every candidate answer record:

- question intent;
- primary Scripture chain;
- countertexts/difficult texts;
- explicit-text conclusions;
- synthesis conclusions;
- inference/tradition labels;
- historical context and source quality where used;
- original-language dependency and source availability where materially relevant;
- pastoral/safety risks;
- known denominational disagreement;
- confidence;
- citations/provenance;
- regression tests that prevent overstatement;
- human-review status.
