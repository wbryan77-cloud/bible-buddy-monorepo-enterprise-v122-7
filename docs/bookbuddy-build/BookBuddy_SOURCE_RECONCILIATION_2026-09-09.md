## Purpose

This document records restoration and governed use of the 20-source BookBuddy upload batch. It does **not** promote any outside source into biblical authority, doctrine, or runtime truth. Scripture remains controlling authority. Modern books are craft/research/reader-psychology references; public-domain philosophy is comparative only; Four Winds is a Scripture-subordinate research/evidence source.

## Routing contract

### BookBuddy may use outside books for

- story and chapter mechanics;
- pacing and reader identification;
- explanation architecture;
- research questions;
- psychology/relationship/financial/grief context after verification;
- comparative philosophy/religion;
- counterexample discovery.

### BibleBuddy may ingest from outside sources only as governed candidates

Required route where applicable:

Scripture → Scripture interpreting Scripture → lesson/source claim → supporting texts → countertexts → history if useful → original-language check when materially necessary → confidence → candidate answer/eval → human review.

Never allow a modern bestseller, philosopher, Four Winds, IOG/ICOJ lesson, Holy Testaments, Wikipedia, historical source, or AI-generated synthesis to override Scripture or silently become doctrine.

## Freeze rule

A source being restored is **not** by itself a reason to rewrite a stable manuscript. Make changes only when source comparison exposes a real gap, error, clarity problem, pacing defect, unsupported claim, reader-safety problem, or missed high-value opportunity. After each edit: render, inspect, retest, and preserve the prior known-good build.
