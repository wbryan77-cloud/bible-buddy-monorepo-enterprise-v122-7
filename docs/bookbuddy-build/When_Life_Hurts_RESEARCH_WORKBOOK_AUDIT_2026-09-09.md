# When Life Hurts — Research / Workbook / Source Integration Audit

Status: DEVELOPMENT / RESEARCH

## Diagnosis
The manuscript did not need arbitrary page inflation. It needed deeper treatment of distinct forms of loss and the movement between grief and ordinary life.

## Research layers added to Book 4 development
- Dual Process Model: oscillation between loss-oriented and restoration-oriented coping.
- Continuing bonds: memory can remain part of adjustment; do not turn psychological continuing-bond language into doctrine that the dead are consciously observing the living.
- Meaning reconstruction: rebuild meaning without inventing God's hidden reason for a loss.
- Ambiguous loss: grief without a clean ending or full absence.
- Disenfranchised grief: breakups, estrangement, pet loss, job/identity loss, church rupture, and other poorly recognized losses.
- Grief neuroscience: attachment, prediction, yearning, triggers, anniversaries, learning a changed reality.
- Prolonged grief: distinguish heavy normal grief from persistent disabling grief that may benefit from specialized care.

## Workbook design extraction
Use a repeated cycle: diagnostic question → short teaching → real-life observation → visual/model if useful → Scripture investigation → embodied/behavioral exercise → concrete next action → optional prayer/conversation/professional-help bridge → later repetition to show change over time.

Benchmark mechanics come from the Whole-Brain Child Workbook (scenario/self-awareness/practice/reflection), Gottman workbooks (quizzes, partner conversations, journals, rituals, conflict scripts), and evidence-based CBT/DBT workbook design (one skill, practice, log, adjust).

## Governance
Human psychology and grief research can explain patterns and offer interventions. It does not control BibleBuddy doctrine. Death-state, resurrection, lament, hope, and theological claims remain Scripture-first and must show countertexts and uncertainty where disputed.
