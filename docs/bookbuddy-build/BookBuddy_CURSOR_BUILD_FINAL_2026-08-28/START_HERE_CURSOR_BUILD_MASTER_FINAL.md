# HOW TO HANDLE "THE BIBLE SAID IT FIRST" CLAIMS

Do not manufacture influence. Similarity is not proof of borrowing.

For every major comparison classify:
- DOCUMENTED_BIBLICAL_INFLUENCE
- STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_HISTORICAL_INFLUENCE
- POSSIBLE_INFLUENCE
- THEMATIC_PARALLEL_ONLY
- GENERAL_ETHICAL_SIMILARITY
- CONTRADICTION
- NO_MEANINGFUL_CONNECTION
- UNKNOWN

Where chronology shows Scripture or a biblical text is earlier, it is safe to say the biblical teaching predates the later text **only when the date comparison itself is well-supported**. Do not automatically say the later writer copied Scripture.

The reader-facing value is often stronger when phrased as:
- “Centuries later, people were still wrestling with the same human problem.”
- “The similarity is real, but the biblical framework grounds the idea differently.”
- “This modern principle resembles part of the biblical pattern, but Scripture adds limits, motives, responsibility, God’s will, covenant, judgment, mercy, or hope that the modern formula does not.”
