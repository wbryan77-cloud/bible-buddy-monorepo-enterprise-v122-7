## Current corpus status
The package now contains 19 active readable source books: 12 public-domain sources and the complete 7-book modern bestseller benchmark set. `Rich Dad Poor Dad — Robert T. Kiyosaki` has been added as a 241-page searchable PDF with a Cursor text derivative.

The duplicate `.lcpl` copy of `How to Win Friends and Influence People` remains excluded because it is LCP-encrypted. A separate readable OCR EPUB of the same title is active.

## Why the package contains text derivatives
EPUB and AZW files are archive/binary ebook formats. Cursor works most reliably when it can index ordinary text/Markdown. Therefore `07_CURSOR_TEXT_DERIVATIVES/` contains Markdown or plain-text derivatives while the original files remain preserved for verification.

For OCR-derived modern books, punctuation, page order, and paragraph metrics must be treated cautiously. Cursor is instructed to flag OCR corruption before making quantitative style claims.

## Strategic diagnosis
The project should not begin by drafting chapters. It should first reverse-engineer the high-level mechanics that make successful books readable and memorable, then combine those mechanics into an original BibleBuddy architecture.

The most important research dimensions are:
1. Story engineering and reader identification.
2. Emotional temperature, kindness, authority, humility, and vulnerability.
3. Pacing and alternation between narrative, principle, evidence, and action.
4. Memorable frameworks and progressive learning.
5. Repetition that reinforces without becoming redundant.
6. Human problems as the entrance into biblical investigation.
7. Scripture as controlling authority with context and Genesis-to-Revelation continuity.
8. Original-language clarification in ordinary English.
9. Comparative religion handled through chronology + evidence, never chronology alone.
10. A data-driven one-book-versus-series decision.

## Story principle
The strongest intended reader movement is:
`ME → MY PROBLEM → MY QUESTION → DISCOVERY → SCRIPTURE → UNDERSTANDING → ACTION → CHANGE`

Stories should be immersive enough to create identification and emotional stakes. They should not be single-sentence placeholders. A chapter may use an opening immersive story, mid-chapter microstories, and a closing return to the opening character.

For copyrighted anecdotes, extract mechanics—not expression. Do not rename characters and reproduce the same story. Create original situations/composites from the structural lessons.

## Direct-from-primary-text translation requirement
BookBuddy must distinguish **reader-facing translation** from **original-language evidence**. When explaining what a biblical word or passage actually says, Cursor must go directly to the governed Hebrew/Aramaic or Koine Greek source, build a token/lemma/morphology/gloss/context chain, and then produce plain English from that primary-language evidence. It must not modernize the KJV and call that an original-language translation, and it must not translate one English translation into another.

The same integrity rule applies to comparative texts when exact wording matters: use Arabic for Qur'anic lexical claims, Pali for Dhammapada claims, Sanskrit for Bhagavad Gita claims, and Classical Chinese for Analects/Tao Te Ching claims. The public-domain English translations remain useful comparison witnesses. If a verified original-language source is unavailable, Cursor must flag the gap rather than reverse-engineering a claim.

## Existing BibleBuddy historical evidence to reuse
Prior BibleBuddy work already identifies `The Last Two Million Years` as an approved historical evidence source and specifically tracks page 87, “Faith survives the dispersion,” Jerusalem/Titus, enslavement, and Mediterranean dispersion. Cursor should locate and reuse those governed records instead of creating a second copy or parallel evidence system.

## Rich Dad Poor Dad added
Treat `Rich Dad Poor Dad` as a major story-engineering benchmark: contrast-pair teaching, mentor/student scenes, dialogue, childhood vulnerability, curiosity through opposing viewpoints, long narrative before principle reveal, memorable lesson labels, and repetition. Its financial/tax/investing claims are not automatically facts; verify any such claims independently before using them in the manuscript.
