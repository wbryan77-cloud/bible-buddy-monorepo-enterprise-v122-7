## High-value mechanics
- Connect emotionally before correction/problem solving.
- Make verbal and nonverbal communication congruent.
- Storytelling can help organize overwhelming experiences.
- Adults should notice/regulate their own triggers before responding.
- Conflict can teach perspective, empathy, repair, and responsibility.
- Repair includes adults apologizing and accepting responsibility.
- SIFT (sensations, images, feelings, thoughts) is an awareness scaffold.
- Healthy family development needs both differentiation and connection.
- Unexamined parental history can intrude into present parenting; reflection can interrupt patterns.
