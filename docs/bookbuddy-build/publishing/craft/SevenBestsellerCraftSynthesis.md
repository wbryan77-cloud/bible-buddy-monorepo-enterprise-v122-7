# Seven-Bestseller Craft Synthesis — BookBuddy House Voice & Writing System

**Status:** Gate 3 craft laboratory output  
**Inputs:** Seven primary `BookDesignProfile`s under `publishing/craft/profiles/`  
**Rule:** Learn mechanics; do not imitate any living (or trademarked) authorial voice. Scripture owns doctrine; these books own *readability craft* only.

## 2. What the seven agree on (transferable)

1. **Name the system.** Portable language beats brilliant unnamed insight.  
2. **Show a human before you lecture.** Even Dummies shows the intimidated beginner’s problem.  
3. **Alternate concrete ↔ abstract.** Story/case → principle → ordinary life.  
4. **Repeat without shame.** Laws, loops, diagrams, nutshells — repetition is pedagogy.  
5. **Give an exit the reader can use tonight.** Homework, checklist, rule card, or map pin.  
6. **Make the book explainable in one sentence.** Recommendability is a design goal.  
7. **Reduce intimidation.** Permission, clarity, and early wins keep pages turning.

## 3. Productive disagreements (BookBuddy choices)

| Tension | Options observed | BookBuddy house choice |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to principle | Clear/Carnegie fast vs Frankl slow | **Hybrid:** fast *orientation principle* + earned *depth principle* after a real human beat |
| Story length | Carnegie shorts vs Duhigg longs vs Frankl mosaic | **Default short-medium** (½–2 pages); reserve long form for rare keystone chapters |
| Tone | Provocation (Kiyosaki) vs solemnity (Frankl) vs jokes (Dummies) | **Warm reverence:** hospitable like Dummies, earnest like Covey, never flip about Scripture, never contemptuous |
| Proof style | Anecdote piles vs lab cases vs witness | **Layered:** recognition story → Scripture → optional historical/research support |
| Practice | Workbook vs understanding-only | **Every major unit ends in action** (Clear/Covey), but action is formation, not lifehack spam |

## 4. Weaknesses we will not inherit

- Corporate seminar density without oxygen (Covey risk).  
- Listicle skimming without transformation (Clear risk).  
- Manipulation vibes / dated social frames (Carnegie risk).  
- Contempt for the “ordinary” reader (Kiyosaki risk).  
- Trauma-as-engagement bait (absolute ban relative to Frankl’s content type).  
- Case bloat that hides the takeaway (Duhigg risk).  
- Flippant sacred tone (Dummies risk).  
- OCR-corrupted sources as quotation authorities (especially Carnegie derivative).

## 5. BookBuddy house voice (original)

**Identity:** A wise friend who has done the homework — pastorally warm, intellectually clear, never salesy, never sneering, never performatively academic.

**Texture:**
- Plain speech first; precise terms second (define once, reuse).  
- Second-person welcome (“you”) without hype.  
- First-person sparingly for humility and solidarity, not brand memoir.  
- Sentences vary: short for stakes; longer for careful theology.  
- Humor: rare, gentle recognition — never punch down, never punch Scripture.

**Emotional temperature:** Warm candle, not spotlight. Hopeful without denial. Serious about sin and suffering without spectacle.

**Authority stack (non-negotiable):**
1. Scripture  
2. Clear reasoning in light of Scripture  
3. Lived human recognition  
4. Historical / research illustration  

Craft bestsellers may influence layers 3–4’s *delivery*, never layer 1’s content.

## 6. BookBuddy chapter contract (writing system)

### Preferred human movement
**LIFE → PAIN → QUESTION → DISCOVERY → SCRIPTURE → UNDERSTANDING → ACTION → CHANGE**

### Default chapter spine
1. **Recognition open** (short lived beat or vivid ordinary scene — original, not borrowed plot).  
2. **Named tension** (the real problem in one plain sentence).  
3. **False path** (what people usually try; why it fails — teach by contrast).  
4. **Discovery bridge** (question that sends reader to Scripture).  
5. **Scripture center** (text leads; we follow).  
6. **Framework crystal** (one diagram, triad, or named tool — original BookBuddy naming).  
7. **Ordinary translation** (home, work, church, mind).  
8. **Action exit** (1–3 concrete practices + optional reflection question).  
9. **Forward hinge** (one sentence toward the next hunger).

### Length & density targets (craft, not legalism)
- One primary job per chapter.  
- One memorable framework artifact max (unless teaching a sequenced set).  
- Stories: usually one keystone + one micro-example.  
- White space: headings every meaningful turn; avoid seminar walls of text.

### Book-level architecture lessons absorbed
- **Covey:** sequence matters (capacity before influence).  
- **Clear:** identity and environment; summaries.  
- **Carnegie:** nutshells; apply tonight.  
- **Kiyosaki:** contrast teaching; sticky diagram.  
- **Frankl:** earned depth; dignity; meaning.  
- **Duhigg:** one model across scales; golden-middle change.  
- **Dummies:** map first; icons for pitfalls/keepers; beginner safety.

## 7. Story policy (house)

Stories are a teaching system, not garnish.

**Allowed pattern library:** see `StorytellingPatternLibrary.md` (mechanics only).  
**Forbidden:** copying anecdotes, distinctive personal histories, trademarked cases, or Holocaust/trauma detail from sources.

**Story → teaching timing:** Prefer story first for recognition; allow principle-first only for orientation/map chapters.

---

## 8. Framework & language devices (house)

Build original, teachable devices:
- Named sequences (3–7 beats).  
- One diagram readers can redraw from memory.  
- Inversions (“to stop X, reverse the same levers”).  
- Callbacks across chapters (shared vocabulary).  
- Part-level nutshells.

Do not clone “7 Habits,” “Four Laws,” “Rich Dad,” “Habit Loop” branding as BookBuddy identity — reinvent mechanics under original names.

---

## 9. Trust & recommendability

A chapter is successful when a reader can tell a friend:
- what human problem it names,  
- what Scripture clarified,  
- what to practice this week,  
- what image/framework to remember.

## 11. Lock statement

BookBuddy’s house voice is **warm-reverent, framework-clear, story-honest, Scripture-first, action-exiting.**  
It is assembled from high-level craft mechanics across the seven — and is intentionally not Covey, Clear, Carnegie, Kiyosaki, Frankl, Duhigg, or For Dummies.
