# Human Connection Scorecard — Seven Craft Benchmarks

**Scale:** 1 = weak / rare · 3 = moderate · 5 = defining strength  
**Method:** Strategic sampling of openings, mid-teaching, and exits from derivatives under `07_CURSOR_TEXT_DERIVATIVES/`. Scores are craft judgments for BookBuddy design — not moral rankings of authors or books.  
**OCR caveats:** Carnegie derivative is heavily corrupted (structure trusted more than tone micro-evidence). Frankl scored on structural/tonal signals without mining traumatic detail. Atomic/Duhigg: minor OCR noise.

**Dimensions**
- **Recognition** — “That’s me” moments  
- **Warmth** — hospitality of tone  
- **Vulnerability** — author/subject risk that builds trust  
- **Hope without denial** — light that doesn’t fake the dark  
- **Dignity** — respect for the reader’s agency and wound  
- **Practical nearness** — can use this tonight  
- **Humor (safe)** — recognition laughter, not contempt  
- **Belonging** — “you’re not alone / you’re invited”

## Evidence notes (mechanics only; no long quotation)

### 7 Habits — Covey
- **Recognition high** where efficiency-vs-people and schedule guilt are named.  
- **Warmth medium:** mentor-earnest more than cozy.  
- **Vulnerability:** admits parental/leadership misfires as teaching fuel.  
- **Practical:** Application Suggestions = connection through usefulness.  
- **Humor low;** seriousness can distance casual readers.  
- **Belonging medium:** “we who want to be principle-centered” tribe, slightly aspirational/exclusive.

### Atomic Habits — Clear
- **Recognition + vulnerability:** personal injury/rebuild open creates instant solidarity, then normalizes tiny starts.  
- **Warmth:** calm coach energy.  
- **Practical max:** summaries/checklists feel like care.  
- **Belonging:** “experiment alongside me” reduces expert distance.  
- **OCR note:** don’t lean on garbled stats for emotional claims.

### How to Win Friends — Carnegie
- **Recognition + belonging max:** packed-hall framing and fearful-speaker sketches say “adults like you are here.”  
- **Warmth/humor:** folksy encouragement (tone inferred despite OCR).  
- **Dignity risk:** influence techniques can tilt manipulative if sincerity drops — score 3.  
- **Practical max:** nutshells + “use immediately.”  
- **OCR:** anecdote wording unreliable; scores emphasize structure.

### Rich Dad Poor Dad — Kiyosaki
- **Recognition:** paycheck/rat-race anxiety lands for many.  
- **Warmth/dignity lower:** contrast engine can shame “poor dad” conventional path.  
- **Vulnerability medium:** confusion/fear present, less tender.  
- **Practical medium:** mindset > stepwise care.  
- **Belonging:** strong for aspiring “rich mindset” in-group; weaker for readers who feel scorned.

### Man’s Search for Meaning — Frankl
- **Vulnerability + dignity + hope:** defining strengths; hope is tragic-aware.  
- **Warmth:** compassion through restraint, not chumminess.  
- **Practical lower:** not a tip book; connection is existential companionship.  
- **Humor minimal** (appropriately).  
- **Ethics:** connection craft here is *presence*, not engagement hacking — BookBuddy must not copy narrative content.

### Power of Habit — Duhigg
- **Recognition:** “I didn’t decide; I looped” insight.  
- **Warmth medium:** reporter distance.  
- **Vulnerability:** mostly subjects’, not author’s.  
- **Practical medium:** model is usable; drills thinner than Clear.  
- **Belonging medium:** curiosity club more than care circle.

### Bible For Dummies — Geoghegan & Homan
- **Warmth + belonging high:** anti-intimidation, icons, “where to go from here.”  
- **Recognition:** lost-beginner problem named early.  
- **Vulnerability low** (guide, not memoir).  
- **Humor:** brand-light; transferable only as gentleness, not jokey sacred tone.  
- **Practical:** orientation tools = care for the overwhelmed.

## BookBuddy design implications

1. **Target band:** Aim for Clear-like practical warmth + Frankl-like dignity/hope + Dummies-like belonging — without Kiyosaki contempt or Dummies flippancy.  
2. **Vulnerability dial:** Prefer Clear-level personal humility *or* composite pastoral scenes; reserve Frankl-level gravity for lament chapters; never spectacle.  
3. **Recognition every chapter:** Name the ordinary failure within the first screen.  
4. **Practical nearness:** End like Covey/Clear/Carnegie (action), even when the middle has Frankl depth.  
5. **Dignity gate:** Before publishing a contrast story, ask whether one “side” is mocked.  
6. **Belonging line:** Explicitly welcome beginners (Dummies) without diluting Scripture authority.

---

## House connection minima (draft standard)

A BookBuddy chapter ships only if:
- Recognition beat exists (score intuition ≥4),  
- Dignity is preserved (no scorn),  
- Hope is present without denial,  
- At least one near action or reflection is offered,  
- Tone would still feel kind if read aloud to a hurting friend.
