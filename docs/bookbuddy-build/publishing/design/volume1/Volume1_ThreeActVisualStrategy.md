# Volume 1 — Three-Act Visual Strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Internal architecture only:** ACT I (ch00–ch05) · ACT II (ch06–ch08) · ACT III (ch09–ch12)  
**Printed rule:** Do **not** show act labels on reader pages unless Founder later approves.

---

## Goal

Support emotional progression through **rhythm, spacing, density, opener depth, callout scarcity, and page-turn placement** — not gimmick font/color changes per act.

---

## ACT I — Recognition (ch00–ch05)

**Feel:** accessible · recognizable · open · inviting · human

| Lever | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Chapter openers | Warm entry; story-first; slightly generous opening depth |
| Density | Moderate; allow early concept furniture without crushing story |
| Callouts | Named frames may appear once per chapter max; keep light |
| Scripture | Clear but not monumental; introduce block style gently |
| White space | After opening disclosures; after “real question” moments |
| Page turns | Prefer turn *into* next life domain, not cliffhanger gimmicks |
| Visual novelty | Lowest — teach the house system here |

**Risk to avoid:** workbook manifesto feel in ch00; table overload in ch03–ch05.

---

## ACT II — Disruption (ch06–ch08)

**Feel:** deeper · quieter · more spacious · more contemplative · more Scripture-centered

| Lever | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Chapter openers | Quieter; more white above first graph; ch06 especially restrained |
| Density | **Reduce** chrome; fewer decorative callouts; longer Scripture breathing |
| Callouts | Near-zero decorative; Job 2:13 / Honest hope only if soft |
| Scripture | Stronger dignity treatment; key discovery passages get room |
| White space | After grief disclosures; before/after Exod 34; between landmark blocks |
| Page turns | Allow short pages / intentional blank verso where grief or wonder needs air |
| Visual novelty | Still same fonts/grid — change *pace*, not costume |

**Risk to avoid:** commercializing Lena; timeline gimmick for G2R; academic OL wall in ch08.

---

## ACT III — Integration (ch09–ch12)

**Feel:** clearer · integrated · purposeful · forward-moving · hopeful

| Lever | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Chapter openers | Slightly firmer hierarchy; still human story first |
| Density | Application may increase — but keep prose-first; cards scarce |
| Callouts | Allow Keepable Rule / Monday Mercy as portable artifacts (1 each) |
| Scripture | Integrated with practice; still dignified, not tip-sheet |
| White space | Protect ch12 quiet last spread; no companion product grid |
| Page turns | Forward energy without cliffhanger salesmanship |
| Visual novelty | Named-frame consistency pays off; do not invent new chrome |

**Risk to avoid:** last-chapter overload; motivational-poster Monday Mercy; C3 as advertisement.

---

## Continuity rules (all acts)

1. Same type family and grid throughout.
2. Same Scripture block grammar throughout.
3. Same (minimal) callout vocabulary throughout.
4. Act difference = **frequency and spacing**, not new brand.
5. Never print “Act I / II / III” in running text or headers.

## Success test

A sensitive designer flipping pages should feel deepening without being able to point to a costume change. A distracted reader should never notice “act design” at all — only that the book gets quieter when pain arrives and clearer when Monday returns.
