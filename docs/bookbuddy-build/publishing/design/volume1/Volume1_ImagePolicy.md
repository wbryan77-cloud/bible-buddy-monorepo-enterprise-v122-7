# Volume 1 — Image Policy

**Date:** 2026-08-29  

---

## Recommendation

**INTERIOR_IMAGE_RECOMMENDATION:** **NO INTERIOR IMAGERY** for Volume 1 first edition.

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| NO INTERIOR IMAGERY | **Recommended** — protects literary tone; lowers cost; avoids AI/stock cliché |
| VERY LIMITED CONCEPTUAL DIAGRAMS | Allowed as *diagrams*, not photos — see Diagram Strategy; Founder approval per diagram |
| SELECTIVE ORIGINAL ART | **Founder approval required** before any commission; not Phase 1/2 default |

## Hard bans

- Stock photography  
- Generic AI illustrations of characters  
- Illustrating every story  
- Soft-focus Jesus / sunset worship clichés  
- Infographic explosion  

## If art is ever considered later

- Not portraits of Maya/Lena/Owen  
- Abstract or typographic chapter ornaments only  
- Must pass copyright originality + non-generic Christian publishing test  

## Success test

Book feels complete without pictures — because type, space, and Scripture already carry meaning.
