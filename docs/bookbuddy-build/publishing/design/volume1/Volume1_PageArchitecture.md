## Trim size

| Candidate | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **6 × 9 in** | Trade standard; room for Scripture blocks + air; commercial expectation | Slightly less portable |
| **5.5 × 8.5 in** | Portable; intimate | Tighter measure; more pages; tables harder |
| 5.25 × 8 | Too tight for this Scripture density | Not recommended |
| 7 × 10 | Textbook risk | Avoid |

**PRIMARY_TRIM_SIZE (RECOMMENDED):** **6 × 9**  
**SECONDARY_TRIM_SIZE (RECOMMENDED):** **5.5 × 8.5** (if Founder prioritizes portability)

### Plausible finished page-count ranges (body + light front/back)

Assumptions: ~300–350 words/page effective with Scripture/air (6×9); denser ~380–420 (not preferred).

| Trim | Lean (denser) | Preferred (breathing) | Generous air |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 × 9 | ~220–260 | **~280–340** | ~350–400 |
| 5.5 × 8.5 | ~260–310 | **~320–390** | ~400–460 |

Front/back matter may add ~12–40 pages depending on notes/indexes.

**ESTIMATED_PAGE_RANGE (headline):** **~280–340 pp at 6×9** with intentional white space.

## Grid / measure (6×9 starting points)

| Spec | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Margins | Outside ≥0.75"; inside/gutter ≥0.85–1.0"; top ≥0.75"; bottom ≥0.85" |
| Line length | ~60–70 characters ideal; avoid >75 |
| Leading | See Typography |
| Paragraph | First-line indent ~0.2–0.3"; **no** extra paragraph gap in continuous prose |
| Section spacing | 8–14 pt before H2; more before major shifts |
| Chapter opening depth | Sink 1/3–1/2 page; story begins below |
| Folio | Bottom center or outer margin |
| Running heads | Outer or center; drop on chapter openers |
| Recto chapter starts | **RECOMMENDED** yes (trade literary) |
| Blank-page policy | Allowed before Act II hinge (ch06) and after peak grief/wonder if needed; not decorative empties |
| Widows/orphans | Strict; no single line stranded |
| Hyphenation | Moderate; limit ladders |
| Rivers | Watch justified setting; prefer quality justification or careful rag |
| Rag | If ragged right, keep even |
| Footnotes | Prefer endnotes for apparatus to protect page rhythm (**RECOMMENDED**) |

## WHITE_SPACE_POLICY

White space is content when it:

- Follows emotional disclosure  
- Precedes major Scripture discovery  
- Follows difficult questions  
- Frames grief (ch06)  
- Frames wonder (ch08 *ḥesed*)  
- Precedes scarce reflection  
- Marks quiet act transitions (without labels)  
- Protects ch12 final literary landing before C3 doorway  

**Do not** maximize words/page merely to cut print cost.  
**Do not** manufacture barren pages for aesthetic pose.

| Zone | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Story immersion | Generous top; no chrome |
| Teaching middle | Normal density; break with subheads + Scripture air |
| Named frame | Small island of space — not a poster board |
| ch06 | Most generous in book |
| ch12 close | Quiet last spread; no companion grid |

## Success test

Pages feel like a trade book: neither pamphlet-sparse nor report-dense.
