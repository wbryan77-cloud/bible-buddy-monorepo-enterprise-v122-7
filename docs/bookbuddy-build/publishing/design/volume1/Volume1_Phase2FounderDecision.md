## Governing lock

| Decision | Lock |
|---|---|
| PRIMARY_DIRECTION | **C — BookBuddy Signature Hybrid** |
| RESTRAINT_GOVERNOR | **A — Quiet Literary** |
| Relationship | C supplies identity; A supplies restraint. **Not** an average of A+C |

### Required feel

human · warm · literary · accessible · Scripture-centered · serious without academic · practical without self-help packaging · designed without over-designed · commercially credible without imitation · distinctly BookBuddy

### Core experience the page must serve

LIFE → PAIN → QUESTION → DISCOVERY → SCRIPTURE → GOD → UNDERSTANDING → CHOICE → FIRST FAITHFUL STEP  

The page serves the encounter. The page is not the encounter.
