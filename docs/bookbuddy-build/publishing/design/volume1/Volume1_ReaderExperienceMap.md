## Book-level reader transform (design checkpoints)

| Stage | Reader should feel |
|---|---|
| ENTRY (ch00) | Seen; invited; not preached at |
| AFTER ACT I | Maybe Scripture understands my life |
| AFTER ACT II | Maybe my God-picture is incomplete — I want to know Him |
| AFTER ACT III | I can begin again Monday; life unfinished and walkable |

## Design implication summary

- **Vary density by chapter** — ch06/ch08 need more air than ch03/ch10.
- **Protect story immersion** at openings — no worksheet chrome over first pages.
- **Reserve stronger visual devices** for named frames, key Scripture discoveries, and scarce reflection — not every H2.
- **Maya bookends** rhyme through editorial rhythm, not illustration.
- **Lena** gets the quietest visual language in the book.
