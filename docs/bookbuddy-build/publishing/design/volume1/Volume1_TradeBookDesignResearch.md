## Scope

Study **design effects** from successful contemporary trade books in faith, personal development, narrative nonfiction, accessible psychology, practical life, and accessible Bible education.

Seven manuscript craft benchmarks remain useful for **reader-effect** analysis only — not trade-dress copying:

- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People  
- Atomic Habits  
- How to Win Friends and Influence People  
- Rich Dad Poor Dad  
- Man’s Search for Meaning  
- The Power of Habit  
- The Bible For Dummies  

**Do not copy:** layouts, trade dress, illustrations, proprietary graphics, distinctive visual branding.

## Principles extracted (effects only)

| Effect | Principle for BookBuddy |
|---|---|
| Page density | Serious narrative nonfiction often runs moderate density with intentional air at emotional peaks — match ch06/ch08 |
| Chapter openers | Literary books sink chapter starts; self-help often shouts — prefer literary sink |
| Heading frequency | Habit books over-head; faith narrative under-head — we have high H2 furniture → demote visually |
| Body readability | Serif long-form wins for 300+ pages |
| Callout restraint | Best-sellers that feel “wise” use few boxes; tip-heavy books use many — we choose few |
| Diagram frequency | Atomic Habits–class books diagram often; Frankl almost never — we default near Frankl, scarce diagrams |
| White space | Grief/meaning books use space as respect |
| Story presentation | Story flush with body type immerses; story-in-shade-box distances |
| Application | Keepable lists beat gamified UI |
| Navigation | Running heads + contents enough; avoid lesson numbering chrome |
| Fatigue | Alternate density; never 20 pages of identical tip cards |

## Competitive positioning (feel)

| Too close to… | Avoid by… |
|---|---|
| Church handout | Kill icon rows, verse boxes everywhere |
| Self-help app book | Kill streak UI, neon accent, card grids |
| Textbook / Dummies | Kill chrome tabs, tip icons, “Remember” boxes |
| Academic monograph | Kill dense notes on live page; demote OL |
| Motivational poster book | Kill giant quotes every chapter |

## Copyright / originality

Benchmark books informed **craft**. They do not provide reader-facing copy or visual identity. Volume 1 must read as its own house.

## Success test

A designer familiar with the benchmarks should recognize *shared reader psychology*, not shared pages.
