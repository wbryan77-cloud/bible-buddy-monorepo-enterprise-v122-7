# Volume 1 — Chapter Opening Strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Must establish:** chapter number · title · optional manuscript-supported epigraph/question · opening story entry · breathing room  
**Must not add:** generic inspirational quotes · benchmark-book quotations · decorative Bible verses to fill empty page

---

## OPTION A — Classic / Literary

**Look:** Folio drop; roman numeral or plain number; title in display serif; generous sink; first paragraph with classic drop cap optional.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Timeless trade credibility | Drop caps can feel costume if overused |
| Protects story entry | May feel “old” to personal-development readers if too austere |

## OPTION B — Modern / Accessible

**Look:** Large sans number; bold title; less sink; possible short deck line.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Easy entry; contemporary | Risks self-help / blog chapter feel |
| Clear navigation | Can overpower quiet stories (Lena) |

## OPTION C — BookBuddy Signature Hybrid (**RECOMMENDED**)

**Look:**
- Small, quiet chapter number (serif or small caps)  
- Title in warm display serif  
- Optional one-line human question *only if already in manuscript opening* — never invented  
- No drop cap by default (Phase 2 may trial on ch00 only)  
- Deep sink on Act II chapters; moderate on Act I/III  
- Immediate story entry; zero callouts on opener spread  

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Literary + accessible | Requires discipline not to drift into Option B chrome |
| Same system series-wide | Needs prototype proof on ch00 vs ch06 |

---

## Per-act opener depth (Hybrid)

| Act | Sink | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| I | Moderate | Invite |
| II | Deep | Quiet, especially ch06 |
| III | Moderate–firm | Forward without shout |

## Success criteria

Opener feels like a book chapter, not a slide title. Reader is inside Maya/Lena/Owen within seconds.
