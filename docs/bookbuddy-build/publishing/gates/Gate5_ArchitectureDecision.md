## Selected architecture

**Flagship + focused companion volumes.**

### Flagship (Volume 1)
**Working title:** *BibleBuddy Life Guide* — *How ordinary people meet God in the middle of real life*

Scope: Mind, purpose, discipline, money entry, relationships entry, suffering entry, knowing God — enough to form a coherent journey without exhausting any domain.

### Companions (later)
1. Money, Work & Prosperity  
2. Relationships & Family  
3. When Life Hurts  
4. Who Is God? (G2R / comparative / historical depth)

## Why this wins
- Lets the seven-bestseller craft lessons drive a readable flagship without forcing 80k+ into every life domain.
- Preserves G2R and Scripture depth without textbook fatigue.
- Matches reader entry (“this sounds like my life”) while allowing companions for deep money/dating/grief/theology.
