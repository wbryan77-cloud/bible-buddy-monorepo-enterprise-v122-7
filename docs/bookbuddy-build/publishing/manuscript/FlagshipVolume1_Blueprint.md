# Flagship Volume 1 Blueprint — BibleBuddy Life Guide

## Promise
Help ordinary readers recognize their life, name the real problem, see why modern advice is attractive, meet Scripture in context with Genesis-to-Revelation continuity, and leave with a believable next step—without sermon tone or self-help-with-verses pasted on.

## Suggested arc (working TOC)

0. Introduction — You are not crazy for wanting a life that works and a God who is real  
1. When your mind won’t quiet down  
2. The person you’re becoming (identity before tactics)  
3. Small faithfulness (discipline without hustle religion)  
4. Money on the kitchen table (stewardship entry)  
5. Who you let close (relationships entry)  
6. When life hurts and tidy answers fail  
7. How to open the Bible without drowning  
8. What God is like from Genesis to Revelation (foundation)  
9. Prayer that tells the truth  
10. A rule of life you can actually keep  
11. Leaving a mark that isn’t a brand  
12. Beginning again on Monday morning
