# Story Brief — What God is like from Genesis to Revelation

- **topic:** Ordinary-reader foundation on God’s character across Scripture (not systematic theology dump)  
- **reader archetype:** Curious or believing person intimidated by “God talk”; carries childhood images + internet arguments + church jargon without a coherent picture  
- **setting:** Friend’s apartment after a dinner that turned into a theology fight; later a quiet drive home; mid-chapter bookstore/café microstory  
- **surface problem:** “I don’t know what God is like—and I’m afraid to admit it.”  
- **deeper wound:** Fear that God is either a soft mascot or a harsh examiner; shame for confusion; hunger for a God who can be trusted without being invented  
- **cultural advice:** Pick a vibe (universe energy / sky grandpa / political mascot / life coach); assemble God from clips; treat doctrine as club membership  
- **misconception:** You must master a system before you may know God; “loving” cancels holiness; “holy” cancels mercy; Jesus is optional garnish on generic spirituality  
- **escalation:** Freezes when asked to describe God in one sentence; later doomscrolls contradictory “God is…” posts; almost quits asking because confusion feels like failure  
- **cost of continuing:** Either a collage-god that cannot judge or save, or a silent exit from the question  
- **choice point:** Let Scripture introduce God on Scripture’s terms across the storyline—not invent a safer deity  
- **biblical question:** Creator, holy/loving, covenant-keeping, judge of evil, merciful, Word made flesh, crucified/risen, coming King—what does the Bible actually say in context?  
- **first action:** Within 24–72 hours: read Exodus 34:1–9 slowly once + John 1:1–18 once; write 5–7 plain sentences: “From these, God is like…” (no jargon contest)  
- **unresolved realism:** Confusion will not vanish in one sitting; one chapter is a doorway, not a doctorate  
- **emotional landing:** Permission to know God as Scripture presents Him—without pretending you already had the system  
- **callbacks (light):** Optional glance at Camila’s beginner-at-the-door courage / Maya’s grocery-list ordinary faith; do not redo those openings  
- **protagonist:** Owen (original; may recur lightly elsewhere)  
- **named frame (max 1):** The God Scripture keeps introducing
