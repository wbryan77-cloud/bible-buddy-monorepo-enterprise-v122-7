# Research Packet — Flagship Ch. “What God is like from Genesis to Revelation”

## Reader question
I hear people talk about God confidently—but when I try to picture who God is, I feel intimidated, confused, or stuck between childhood images and internet arguments. What is God actually like according to the Bible’s whole story?

## Primary Scripture candidates (key passages + immediate context)

- Exodus 34:6–7 — merciful and just self-revelation (with golden-calf context)
- Leviticus / holiness thread light — holy (not merely “nice”)
- Deuteronomy / covenant reminders — covenant-keeping
- Psalm 103 / Jonah 4 light — merciful character contested by human anger
- Prophets on justice — judges evil (e.g. Amos / Isaiah beat)
- John 1:1–18 — Word made flesh

- Revelation 19–22 — coming King / making all things new (select lightly)
