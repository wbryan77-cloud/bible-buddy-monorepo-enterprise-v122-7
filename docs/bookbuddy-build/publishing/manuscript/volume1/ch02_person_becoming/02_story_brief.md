# Story Brief — The person you’re becoming (identity before tactics)

- **topic:** Identity / purpose before tactics; received self under God vs self-salvation by rebrand  
- **reader archetype:** Achiever who keeps reinventing “who I am”; exhausted by identity projects; secretly afraid of being ordinary  
- **setting:** Mirror and closet before a networking event; phone notes titled with versioned identity statements; later a quiet Sunday afternoon  
- **surface problem:** “I still don’t know who I am / what I’m becoming”  
- **deeper wound:** Treating the self as a product to launch; fear that without a brand-story you are unlovable or useless  
- **cultural advice:** Manifest the new you; curate a personal brand; “become the type of person”; purpose as product; aesthetic conversion  
- **misconception:** Christian identity is just nicer self-help; new creation = vibe shift you declare into being  
- **escalation:** Builds Identity 4.0 plan (career pivot + aesthetic + mantra); performs it for two weeks; crashes into old patterns; shame spiral—“I can’t even become myself”  
- **cost of continuing:** Endless remakes; contempt for the present self; tactics without belonging  
- **choice point:** Receive identity under God before optimizing the next version  
- **biblical question:** What do Gen 1 image, Rom 12, Eph 4, and 2 Cor 5 say about who you are and how you change?  
- **first action:** Write “received” vs “performed” lists; one belonging prayer; one next faithful act without rebrand theater  
- **unresolved realism:** Still will feel foggy about vocation; still will need tactics later  
- **emotional landing:** Dignity as creature and new creation; becoming as formation, not product launch
