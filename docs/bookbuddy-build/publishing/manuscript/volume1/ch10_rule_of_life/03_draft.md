# Chapter — A rule of life you can actually keep

(Gate 8 controlled draft — expand to full freeze as `04_FULL.md`)

Marisol’s Sunday night calendar looked holy and already doomed.

Color blocks for prayer, fasting, journaling, gym, Scripture plans, two groups, and a 5 a.m. “monastery hour.” By Wednesday the blocks had become guilt. By Friday she almost deleted every spiritual app and called it honesty.

She did not want legalism. She also did not want drift that felt like freedom until Thursday felt empty.

*Is there a rule of life I can actually keep?*

---

## What are you really asking?

- Is structure legalism?  
- Is rest laziness?  
- Does God only love impressive schedules?  
- How do prayer, Scripture, people, rest, and work live together under God?

---

## You’ve probably heard this before

Habit culture notices rhythm—**THEMATIC_PARALLEL**. Manifestation calendars and productivity-as-righteousness **CONTRADICT** Scripture. Monastic overload for non-monastics used as shame: refuse. Structureless drift as “freedom”: refuse.

---

## What Scripture actually says

Sabbath rest themes; Mark 1:35 (Jesus prays amid ministry); Acts 2:42 (teaching, fellowship, bread, prayers). Context required. Reject hustle-gospel cousin of Ch03.

Frame: **The Keepable Rule.**

---

## What this does not mean

- Not anti-effort.  
- Not monastic cosplay.  
- Not no-structure spirituality.  
- Not manifestation.
