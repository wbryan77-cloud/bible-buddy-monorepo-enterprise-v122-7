# Story Brief — A rule of life you can actually keep

- **topic:** Practical pattern of prayer / Scripture / community / rest / work under God — anti-hustle, anti-legalism  
- **reader archetype:** Person who either builds cathedral schedules that collapse by Wednesday or refuses structure until life thins out  
- **setting:** Sunday night kitchen with color-coded calendar apps open; later a midweek crash; microstory of a friend who quit “spiritual systems” entirely  
- **surface problem:** “I can’t keep a spiritual routine.”  
- **deeper wound:** Confusing worth with schedule perfection; fear that God only loves impressive discipline; hunger for a life with shape that still has mercy  
- **cultural advice:** Optimize like a monk; hustle like a brand; manifest the ideal day; or burn the calendar and call drift “freedom”  
- **misconception:** Rule of life = legalism; OR rule of life = personality makeover; OR rest is laziness; OR community is optional garnish  
- **escalation:** Prints a seven-tab “Rule of Life 3.0”; fails by day four; shame-spirals; nearly abandons prayer/Scripture altogether  
- **cost of continuing:** Either crushing overload or structureless emptiness  
- **choice point:** Trade spectacle-systems for a keepable rule under God—small, honest, communal enough, restful enough  
- **biblical question:** What do Sabbath, Mark 1:35, Acts 2:42, and related rhythms teach about ordered life without self-salvation?  
- **first action:** Within 24–72 hours: write a one-page Keepable Rule with five lines (pray / Scripture / people / rest / work) — each doable in ordinary week; practice for 72 hours; confess misses without quitting  
- **unresolved realism:** The week may still be messy; one rule will need revision; feelings may lag practice  
- **emotional landing:** Permission to live a shaped life that is neither monastery theater nor unsupervised drift  
- **callbacks (light):** Lena’s “next faithful inch”; Naomi’s honest prayer; Camila’s beginner door — do not redo openings  
- **protagonist:** Marisol (original; may recur lightly elsewhere)  
- **named frame (max 1):** The Keepable Rule  

**Status:** READY TO DRAFT
