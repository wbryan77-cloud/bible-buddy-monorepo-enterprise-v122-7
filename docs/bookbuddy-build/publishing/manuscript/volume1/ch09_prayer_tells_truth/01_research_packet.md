# Research Packet — Flagship Ch. “Prayer that tells the truth”

## Reader question
I know how to sound spiritual when I pray—but I don’t know how to tell God the truth. Can honest prayer (lament, confession, need) really be holy?

## Felt problem
Performance prayer; fear that raw emotion offends God; polished church language that never reaches the wound; silence when life is messy; confusing “faith” with pretending everything is fine.

- Frankl dignity under suffering (honesty without exploitation)

## Primary Scripture candidates
- Psalms of lament (e.g. Psalm 13; Psalm 42 / honesty of soul before God)
- Matthew 6:5–13 — Lord’s Prayer portion (against performance; toward Father)
- Hebrews 4:14–16 — throne of grace; boldness / sympathy of High Priest
- Philippians 4:6–7 — requests made known (light touch; do not rehash entire mind/anxiety chapter)
- Luke 18:9–14 — Pharisee and publican (performed righteousness vs honest mercy-plea)

## G2R angle
From Genesis, humans speak to God (and hide); Israel cries out in Egypt; Psalms teach a vocabulary of praise *and* lament; prophets intercede; Jesus prays honestly in Gethsemane and teaches the disciples to pray; the church approaches the throne through Christ; Revelation’s prayers of the saints rise before God.
