# Story Brief — Leaving a mark that isn’t a brand

- **topic:** Impact / legacy under God — service, salt/light, prepared good works, shepherding ethic, generosity — anti-influencer-gospel  
- **reader archetype:** Person who wants to matter and keeps measuring “matter” by platform, résumé, or applause  
- **setting:** Nonprofit gala afterparty / parking garage; later a Tuesday tutoring room; microstory of a quiet coworker who leaves without a farewell post  
- **surface problem:** “I want to leave a legacy / make an impact.”  
- **deeper wound:** Fear of invisibility; baptizing ambition as calling; confusing brand with fruit; hunger to be remembered more than to love a neighbor  
- **cultural advice:** Build personal brand; document every good deed; optimize reach; treat legacy like a product launch  
- **misconception:** Faithfulness requires followers; salt must be spicy content; light = spotlight; good works = self-salvation; shepherding = platform authority  
- **escalation:** Drafts a “legacy statement” for a career workshop; posts a service reel that gets weak engagement; spirals into “maybe I’m not called”; nearly quits a quiet tutoring commitment because it doesn’t “scale”  
- **cost of continuing:** Either hollow ambition theater or cynical withdrawal from ordinary good  
- **choice point:** Trade branded legacy for prepared good works and servant greatness—one unphotographed act that loves a real person  
- **biblical question:** What do Matt 5:13–16, Eph 2:10, 1 Pet 5, and Matt 20:25–28 teach about mark-making under God?  
- **first action:** Within 24–72 hours: name one prepared work already in front of you (not a brand plan); do it without posting; tell one trusted person only if accountability helps, not for applause  
- **unresolved realism:** The desire to be seen may return; the quiet work may stay quiet; feelings may lag obedience  
- **emotional landing:** Permission to leave a mark that salt leaves—flavor and preservation—without needing a logo  
- **callbacks (light):** Optional glance at Lena’s next faithful inch / Marisol’s keepable rule / Tessa’s beloved-before-brand — do not redo openings  
- **protagonist:** Helen (original; may recur lightly elsewhere)  
- **named frame (max 1):** The Unbranded Mark
