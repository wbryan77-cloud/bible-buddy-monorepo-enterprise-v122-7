# Research Packet — Flagship Ch. “Leaving a mark that isn’t a brand”

## Reader question
I want my life to matter—but every “legacy” script I hear sounds like personal branding with a halo. Is there a Christian way to leave a mark that isn’t influencer-gospel?

## Felt problem
Ambition that tastes holy and hollow; comparison with platformed faith; fear of dying unnoticed; confusing visibility with fruit; shame for wanting impact; empty “make a difference” slogans with no neighbor attached.

## Craft lessons to apply
- Recognition before lecture; delayed principle after lived cost
- One original BookBuddy frame (max 1 primary)
- Reject influencer-gospel / legacy-as-brand / empty ambition
- Story open + mid microstory + “what this does not mean” + story return
- Four Winds unused; OL only if load-bearing

## Primary Scripture candidates
- Matthew 5:13–16 — salt and light (Sermon on the Mount context: discipleship, persecution, righteousness—not platform growth)
- Ephesians 2:10 — created in Christ Jesus unto good works which God prepared (context: grace/faith, not works-salvation; works as prepared path)
- 1 Peter 5:1–4 — shepherds feed the flock, not for filthy lucre or as lords (context: elders; transferable ethic of serving under the Chief Shepherd)
- Matthew 20:25–28 — greatness as servant / Son of man came to minister (context: rivalry of the disciples after mother’s request)
- Optional light: Galatians 6:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; John 13:12–17
