## 4. Why seven-benchmark composite ~88 while master QA is 94

These measure different things.

- **Master QA (94):** internal BookBuddy manuscript health (promise, Scripture integrity, voice, ending, originality, readiness).  
- **Seven-benchmark composite (~88):** how closely V1 produces the *reader effects* of seven commercial craft champions—without becoming them.

### Per benchmark

**1. 7 Habits — Covey** · Score **86**  
CRAFT EFFECT: coherent worldview progression; principle deepening.  
DOES WELL: private formation → domains → God → keepable life; portable frames.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: fewer memorable matrices/diagrams; tables sometimes feel like workbook not worldview artifact.  
IMPROVE? Desirable lightly via design diagrams—not more seminar prose.  
DAMAGE HOUSE VOICE if forced into Covey seminar tone? **Yes** if overdone.

**2. Atomic Habits — Clear** · Score **90**  
CRAFT EFFECT: low friction; named ideas; immediate usability.  
DOES WELL: inch / Keepable Rule / Monday Mercy; identity-before-tactics.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: chapter density still above Clear’s one-idea crispness.  
IMPROVE? Yes via further compression in ch01/ch08—not new systems.  
DAMAGE? Low if we shorten, not if we sloganize.

**3. How to Win Friends — Carnegie** · Score **88**  
CRAFT EFFECT: anecdote density; principle after story.  
DOES WELL: story-first opens; warm address.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: fewer short proof anecdotes per principle than Carnegie.  
IMPROVE? Desirable if adding *small* micro-proofs—not more lectures.  
DAMAGE? Low.

**4. Rich Dad Poor Dad — Kiyosaki** · Score **87**  
CRAFT EFFECT: contrast; dialogue; delayed principle.  
DOES WELL: steward vs claimant; brand vs beloved; slogan vs honest hope.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: one unforgettable binary diagram across whole book still softer than money chapter alone.  
IMPROVE? Design can elevate contrasts.  
DAMAGE? If we force false binaries everywhere—yes.

**5. Man’s Search — Frankl** · Score **92**  
CRAFT EFFECT: dignity; earned meaning; restraint.  
DOES WELL: ch06; honest hope; anti-slogan suffering.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: other chapters rarely risk that gravity.  
IMPROVE? Only carefully—don’t fake trauma.  
DAMAGE? High if we manufacture pain.

**6. Power of Habit — Duhigg** · Score **86**  
CRAFT EFFECT: case-study revelation of mechanism.  
DOES WELL: Andre scene; anxiety-as-care; performance prayer.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: still fewer long detective case runways; template often replaces case.  
IMPROVE? Highest craft ROI if Founder wants another pass—**one** deeper case in ch08 or money.  
DAMAGE? Low if cases stay original.

**7. Bible For Dummies** · Score **90**  
CRAFT EFFECT: accessibility; navigation; no condescension.  
DOES WELL: ch07 door; plain English after scrub.  
KEEPS FROM 90+: remaining list maps; intro still long.  
IMPROVE? Design icons/sidebars.  
DAMAGE? If we become flippant—yes.

### Why 88 vs 94?

**Answer: D — combination.**

- **A (genuine weakness):** residual template H2 furniture; mid-book density; fewer Duhigg-length cases.  
- **B (intentional difference):** we refuse miracle endings, prosperity promises, and trademarked systems; we keep KJV/context seriousness Frankl-adjacent more than Clear-light.  
- **C (scoring artifact):** benchmark composite penalizes not being seven different bestsellers at once; master QA rewards *our* promise integrity.

**Remaining useful craft lesson (no copying):**  
Break remaining identical H2 spines in 3–4 mid chapters **or** convert remaining comparison tables into design artifacts—so the reader stops predicting the machinery. That is the clearest path from craft ~88 → ~90 without abandoning house voice.
