# Story Brief — How to open the Bible without drowning

- **topic:** Beginning Bible reading without intimidation  
- **reader archetype:** Curious / returning / ashamed-of-not-knowing adult  
- **setting:** Apartment evening; unread Bible app + paper Bible on shelf  
- **surface problem:** “I don’t know where to start”  
- **deeper wound:** Fear of being spiritually stupid; fear God is disappointed  
- **cultural advice:** Random verse apps; “just start at Genesis and force it”; study-Bible overwhelm; podcasts instead of Scripture  
- **misconception:** Real Christians never feel lost in the text  
- **escalation:** Starts Genesis, hits genealogies, quits, shame spiral  
- **cost of continuing:** Secondhand faith only; fragile hope  
- **choice point:** Ask for a readable path, not a performance  
- **biblical question:** How does God meet people who need the Scriptures opened?  
- **first action:** Read one short Gospel episode aloud + one Psalm; ask one question  
- **unresolved realism:** Still won’t know everything tomorrow  
- **emotional landing:** Relief that seeking understanding is biblical  
- **callback:** Maya from Introduction’s unread Bible
