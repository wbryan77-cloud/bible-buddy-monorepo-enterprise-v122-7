## Where we go from here (one page)

You finished a book that kept a modest promise: take your real life seriously, open Scripture in context, refuse slogan gods, and return on Monday under mercy that is new.

That promise does not include a painless life.

If, after Monday Mercy, you still find yourself in a waiting room, a chip aisle you hate, a voicemail you fear returning, or a silence where slogans used to live—you are not failing the flagship. You are meeting the next honest human question:

**What do I do when life still hurts?**

Scripture already opened that door in Job’s protest, the Psalms’ roar, Jesus’ tears at a grave, and the long hope of Romans 8. A later companion can sit there longer—with new stories, not recycled ones—without pretending the flagship left you unfinished on purpose.

Until then: keep the walk you already began. Mercies are still new. The Word is still foundation. The ache does not get to be god.
