# BookBuddy Chapter Contract (locked)

## Length
- **Full freeze target:** **3,200–4,800 words** per chapter.
- **Pilot / first-draft band:** **1,200–2,200 words** allowed if structure, story return, Scripture context, “does not mean,” and next step are complete — then expand to full freeze in Gate 8 before series release.
- Intro full target: **1,800–2,800 words** (pilot intro may sit ~1,000–1,500 first).

## Mix (approximate)
- Story / human scene: **35–45%**
- Teaching / Scripture / G2R: **40–50%**
- Action / reflection: **10–15%**

## Concept load
- Max **3 new named ideas** per chapter.
- Prefer one memorable frame; deepen later.

## Opening modes (allowed)
1. Immersive original story  
2. Urgent real-life question + brief scene  
3. Competing cultural answers (then story)

## Recurring moves (not mandatory headers every time)
- Real life  
- What are you really asking?  
- You’ve probably heard this before  
- What Scripture actually says (with context)  
- What the word actually says (original language when material)  
- Line upon line / Genesis to Revelation  
- What this does not mean  
- Monday morning / what to do now  
- Story return

## Scripture rules
- Immediate context required.
- Distinguish EXPLICIT vs inference.
- G2R: at least one continuity link beyond the primary passage for major claims.
- Original language: only when it changes meaning; use governed OSHB/Nestle1904 chain.

## Evidence / comparison
- Modern ideas: paraphrase high-level; cite; no long copyrighted excerpts.
- Comparative/philosophy: chronology ≠ borrowing.
- Four Winds: VERIFIED/QUALIFIED only.

## Exit
- One concrete next step doable within 24–72 hours.
- Optional short reflection/prayer (not a sermon altar call paste).

Per `SevenBestsellerCraftSynthesis.md` house voice.
