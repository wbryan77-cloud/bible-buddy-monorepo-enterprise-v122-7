## Series ending rule (governs EVERY volume)

**COMPLETE THE PRESENT PROMISE.**  
**SURFACE THE NEXT HUMAN NEED.**  
**CONNECT IT THROUGH SCRIPTURE.**  
**CREATE CURIOSITY WITHOUT MANIPULATION.**  
**LET THE NEXT BOOK FEEL NECESSARY BECAUSE LIFE ITSELF CREATED THE QUESTION.**

Each volume must fully deliver on its own central promise. The reader must never feel the present book withheld its answer merely to sell another book.

After that promise is fulfilled, the ending may naturally reveal the next important human question. Desired experience:

1. “I received something complete and useful from this book.”  
2. Then: “But this has opened another question in my life that I now want to understand.”

Required anatomy (execution must vary; never clone the same device):

```
BOOK_RESOLUTION
→ HUMAN_AFTERSHOCK
→ NEXT_QUESTION
→ SCRIPTURAL_BRIDGE
→ NEXT_VOLUME_DOORWAY
```

Prefer a human story moment over a promotional teaser. No melodramatic cliffhangers. No sequel advertisements inside the literary ending. Optional separable previews may exist as publishing elements only if they do not weaken the literary close.
